Queestion 6: What will be the suitable Output of the Following Programme:

text= "banana"
result=text.strip("ab")
print(result)

A. "banana"
B. "nan"
C. "nana"
D. Argument Error

Correct Answer: B

Explaination: strip(ab) means doing striping on the basises of a and b, means when you run text.strip("ab")
the output will be nan.
